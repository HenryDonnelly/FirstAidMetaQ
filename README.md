# QuestQCPR

## Overview
QuestQCPR is an augmented reality app designed to improve CPR training classes while also providing the option to train from home. It is built for the Meta Quest 3, and as a result of developing using Meta SDK software, it is not compatable with other devices (such as Sony or Apple).
The APK is publicly available for download on the apps website. You can see the website for this app [here](https://first-aid-site-eight.vercel.app/).

## Installation
To use the app on your Meta Quest 3, Simply navigate to the apps website and click download (which will bring you to a google drive downloads page).
After downloading on your headset, select the app from your downloads menu and choose "open with package installer".

## Use guide
There is a demonstration on the webpage on using the app. However, its not essential to watch as there is a tutorial when starting up the app. The most important thing to remember is that to bring up the app menu, face your left hand towards you and pinch your index and thumb together. 
Gesturing with the inverse hand brings up the Meta menu.
